The Story

Why Readerly Exists

Good books changed how I think about money, business, and life. But in Nigeria, getting the right book at the right price is harder than it should be.

Readerly is my answer to that. I source the best self-development books so you don't have to hunt for them. And if you're in Enugu, you can rent instead of buying, so one book can change your life without emptying your pocket.

Every book in this catalog is one I've personally read or carefully selected. This isn't a random store, it's a curated library built by someone who actually reads.

~ Governor, Founder of Readerly
